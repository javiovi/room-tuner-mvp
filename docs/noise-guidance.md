# Scope of the noise guidance

Noise advice uses user-declared source and entry path. The app does not detect leaks,
measure isolation or determine transmission through the building. An unknown path
never implies an indoor source or a product prescription.

For windows and doors, closure comparisons precede conditional advice about seals.
A seal is relevant only to an actual gap; the complete assembly and other paths may
remain limiting. For walls, floors, ceilings and vibration, do not substitute foam,
curtains or a weatherstrip for an assessment of the assembly. Maintain ventilation
and usable exits. No promised reduction in dB or percentage is attached to advice.

The same conditional guidance is used in the interface and PDF. The general acoustic
product catalog is not shown as an isolation plan for a noise concern. Sources support
the physical principles, not the performance of a particular intervention in a user's
room; that remains unverified.

Reviewed sources:
- Australian Government, YourHome, Noise control: https://www.yourhome.gov.au/live-adapt/noise-control
- New Zealand MBIE, Reducing noise: https://www.building.govt.nz/getting-started/smarter-homes-guides/design/reducing-noise
